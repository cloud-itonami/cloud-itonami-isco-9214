# Business Model: Garden and Horticultural Site Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9214`
- ISCO-08: `9214`
- Occupation: Garden and Horticultural Labourers
- Social impact: worker-safety, green-space-quality, production-continuity

## Customer

- parks / grounds / botanical-garden and landscaping operators
- independent garden and horticultural-site crews / labour cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- planting/maintenance-log/progress-record logging
- plants/soil/tool-supplies procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to site safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a garden-work-execution decision (e.g.
  finalizing the garden-maintenance operation), ever
- no direct finalization of a site-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring the site safety cleared), ever
- no override of a site safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (power-tool hazard, pesticide/fertilizer
  chemical-exposure hazard, outdoor weather-exposure hazard) always
  route to human sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
